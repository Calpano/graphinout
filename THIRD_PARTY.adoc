(file-level) copyleft:

* *Apache-2.0* — permissive (Apache License 2.0); keep notices, state changes; includes a patent grant.
* *MIT* — permissive; do anything, keep the copyright and license notice.
* *BSD-3-Clause* — permissive; like MIT, plus you may not use the authors' names to endorse derivatives.
* *EPL-2.0* — weak copyleft (Eclipse Public License 2.0); share changes to covered files, may be combined into larger works.
* *EPL-1.0* — weak copyleft; the older Eclipse Public License.
* *LGPL-2.1* — weak copyleft (GNU Lesser GPL 2.1); link freely, but share changes to the library itself.
* *MPL-1.1* — weak copyleft (Mozilla Public License 1.1); per-file reciprocity, combinable with other code.
* *CDDL-1.1* — weak copyleft (Common Development and Distribution License 1.1); Mozilla-style per-file reciprocity.
* *GPL-2.0+CPE* — copyleft (GPL v2) with the Classpath Exception; independent modules may link it without becoming GPL.
