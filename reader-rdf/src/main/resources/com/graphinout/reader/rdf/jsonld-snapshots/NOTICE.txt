Bundled JSON-LD context snapshots
=================================

The files in this folder are unmodified copies of published JSON-LD context documents, bundled so that JSON-LD input
using them can be read without network access. index.txt lists, per file, its SHA-256 hash, retrieval date, and the
URLs it is served for. They remain under their publishers' licenses:

schema.org.jsonld
  Source:  https://schema.org/docs/jsonldcontext.jsonld
  License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 (CC BY-SA 3.0), https://schema.org/docs/terms.html

w3.org-2018-credentials-v1.jsonld, w3.org-ns-credentials-v2.jsonld, w3.org-ns-activitystreams.jsonld,
w3.org-ns-did-v1.jsonld, w3id.org-security-data-integrity-v2.jsonld, w3id.org-security-multikey-v1.jsonld
