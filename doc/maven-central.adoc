= Maven Central Publishing Limits

== The Problem

Maven Central is introducing per-organization, per-month publishing limits.
Our current release *exceeds two of the three limits*, which means that once
enforcement begins we will no longer be able to publish.

Reference: https://central.sonatype.org/publish/maven-central-publishing-limits/

== Enforcement Timeline

* *June 16, 2026* — Soft limits begin: warnings shown at 75% and 90% of each limit.
* *August 11, 2026* — Hard enforcement: publishing is *blocked* once a limit is exceeded.

Limits are tracked monthly per organization and are visible in the Usage Center
at https://central.sonatype.com/publishing/usage.

== What We Need to Do

To keep publishing after August 11, 2026, we must do at least one of the following:

. *Reduce release size* below 15 MB (currently 0.95 MB over).
. *Reduce file count* below 300 per release (currently 480 files over — the biggest gap).
. *Request an exemption* — community open-source projects with unusual publishing
  patterns can email central-support@sonatype.com with namespace, organization,
  project description, and an explanation of the publishing pattern. Reviewed case-by-case.
. *Subscribe to Maven Central Publisher Pro* (paid) for increased capacity.

The most pressing issue is the *file count (260% of limit)*: we publish 780 files
per release against a 300-file cap.
